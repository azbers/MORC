DAUR SUPPLY  |  LOGO FILES
Made 29 October 2026 from the final DAUR Supply marks (loop D symbol and
the drawn DAUR wordmark, brand/daur-*.svg in the azbers/MORC repository).

TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND
Every file in "SVG", "PNG 4000px" and "PNG 1200px (web)" has a transparent
background. "With Background" holds the square app icon on a solid colour.

LAYOUTS
  Symbol     the loop D alone (profile pictures, favicon, stamps, watermark)
  Wordmark   DAUR alone (lace tag, engraving, small spaces)
  Lockup     DAUR with the brass rule and SUPPLY underneath (website header)
  Stacked    symbol over DAUR and SUPPLY (box lid, cover images, print)

COLOURS
  Espresso   #2A1C14 with brass #B0843F accent   for light backgrounds
  Hide       #F5EEE4 with brass #C89B5E accent   for dark backgrounds
  Brass      #B0843F                              on espresso or black
  Black      #000000                              one colour print, engraving
  White      #FFFFFF                              on photos, dark print

WHICH FILE TO USE
  Printing, laser engraving, box, signage   SVG (scales to any size)
  Canva, PowerPoint, Word, social designs   PNG 4000px
  Website, email, WhatsApp                  PNG 1200px (web)
The SUPPLY text inside the SVG files is converted to shapes, so the files
look the same on any computer without the JetBrains Mono font.

RULES
  * Keep clear space around the mark of at least the height of the D's
    bottom bar.
  * Do not stretch, recolour outside the palette, add shadows or outlines.
  * DAUR alone on the lace tag and on the logo; "DAUR Supply" in text.
  * No trademark search has been done yet. Run a WIPO image search and ask a
    trademark agent before printing boxes or tags in volume.
