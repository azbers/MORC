DAUR SUPPLY  |  WORD LIST (marketing, ads, store and shipping)

Ad account        the Meta account that pays for ads, inside the Business Portfolio.
Ad set            the middle level of a Meta ad: audience, placements, budget.
Advantage+        Meta's automatic setting that lets its system choose audience
                  or placements. Recommended for small budgets.
Aglet             the plastic or metal tip at the end of a lace.
AOV               average order value: total sales divided by number of orders.
Bleed             extra artwork (usually 3 mm) beyond the cut line so no white edge
                  shows after cutting. Printers ask for it.
Business Portfolio  Meta's name (formerly Business Manager) for the folder that holds
                  Page, Instagram, WhatsApp, ad account, Pixel and people.
Carousel          one post with several swipeable images or videos.
CMYK              the four printing inks. Screens use RGB; print files must be CMYK.
COD               Cash on Delivery. The customer pays the rider.
Conversion        the action you want: a chat, an order, a purchase.
Conversions API   server to server tracking between Shopify and Meta; works even
  (CAPI)          when a browser blocks the Pixel.
CPM               cost per 1,000 impressions.
CTR               click through rate: clicks divided by impressions.
CTWA              Click to WhatsApp ad.
Dieline           the flat drawing of a box with cut and fold lines.
Engagement        likes, comments, shares, saves.
Fit note          DAUR's name for a customer review.
Frequency         average times one person saw your ad.
Hook              the first 1 to 3 seconds of a Reel or ad that make people stop.
Impressions       times an ad or post was shown.
Learning phase    the first days of an ad set while Meta learns who responds.
Lookalike         an audience Meta builds that resembles your customers.
Organic           unpaid reach from posts and Reels.
Pantone           a standard numbered ink colour system used by printers.
Pixel             a piece of Meta code on the website that reports visits and
                  purchases back to Meta.
Reach             number of different people who saw something.
Reel              a vertical short video on Instagram or Facebook.
Refusal / RTO     a COD parcel the customer did not accept; it returns to origin.
Retargeting       showing ads to people who already visited, chatted or engaged.
ROAS              return on ad spend: sales value divided by ad spend.
Sends             people sharing your post in a DM. A strong signal for Instagram.
SKU               stock keeping unit: one model, in one colour, in one size.
Story             a vertical post that disappears after 24 hours.
UGC               user generated content: photos or videos made by customers.
Volumetric weight courier billing weight from box size: length x width x height in
                  cm divided by a number the courier sets (often 5,000). A shoe box
                  can be billed heavier than it weighs. Ask each courier.
