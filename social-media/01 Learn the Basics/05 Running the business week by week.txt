DAUR SUPPLY  |  RUNNING THE BUSINESS, DAY BY DAY AND WEEK BY WEEK
Written 29 October 2026 for an owner with a day job.

EVERY DAY (about 30 to 45 minutes, split morning and evening)
  [ ] Morning: reply to every WhatsApp chat, Instagram DM and Facebook message.
  [ ] Confirm new orders (see 03 Selling on WhatsApp with COD.txt).
  [ ] Book confirmed orders in the courier app; print the packing slip
      (Order Printer) and the courier label.
  [ ] Pack: relace with the black laces, brass tag, seal on the top eyelet,
      tissue, Manifest Card, box, courier label.
  [ ] Hand parcels to the rider or drop at the branch.
  [ ] Send tracking numbers.
  [ ] Evening: post the day's Story or Status (04 Platforms, today's folder);
      feed posts go out from Postiz by themselves once it is connected.
  [ ] Reply to comments.

EVERY WEEK (about 1 hour, pick a fixed day, for example Sunday)
  [ ] Fill the Weekly sheet in 01 Learn the Basics/DAUR Numbers.xlsx.
  [ ] Check the courier payout matches the delivered COD orders.
  [ ] Count stock per model and size. Mark sold out sizes in Shopify.
  [ ] Approve or reject new Fit notes (Shopify > Content > Metaobjects).
  [ ] Open Instagram Insights: which post got the most shares and profile
      visits. Make more like it.
  [ ] Send the Weekly sheet and Insights screenshots to Claude.
  [ ] Prepare next week's Reels (film on the weekend).

EVERY MONTH
  [ ] Month review in 04 Platforms/Meta Ads (Day 30 folder).
  [ ] Set aside the charity pledge: at least 5% of the month's profit.
  [ ] Reorder fast selling sizes; drop slow ones.
  [ ] Reorder packaging before it runs out (printers often need 1 to 3 weeks).
  [ ] Back up: export Shopify orders (Orders > Export) to this computer.

SIMPLE BOOKKEEPING (no software needed at the start)
Keep one sheet with every money movement: date, what, money in, money out.
Keep every invoice photo (workshop, printer, courier, Meta receipts) in one
folder per month. This is what an accountant or FBR will ask for.

STOCK RULES
  * Sell only what is in your hands or confirmed by the vendor for same week
    delivery. The project memory notes an open question (Tirmidhi 1232) about
    selling what you do not yet hold; ask a qualified mufti, as planned.
  * Keep the stock count in Shopify true. Overselling creates refused and
    cancelled orders.

QUALITY RULE
The website promises hand checks of stitching, grain, upper flex, sole bond,
and comfort, with a pull test at toe and heel. Do them on every pair, every
time, or change the website text.
