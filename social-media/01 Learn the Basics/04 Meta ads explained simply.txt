DAUR SUPPLY  |  META ADS EXPLAINED SIMPLY
Written 29 October 2026. The exact campaign settings for each day are in
"04 Platforms/Meta Ads". This file explains what the words mean.

1. WHERE ADS LIVE
  Meta Business Suite / Business Portfolio  the company folder. Holds your
      Facebook Page, Instagram account, WhatsApp number, ad account, Pixel and
      the people who can use them. Set up in "02 Business and Meta Setup".
  Ads Manager (adsmanager.facebook.com)  where you build, run and read ads.
  Do not use the blue "Boost post" button for sales. It has fewer settings.
  Build ads in Ads Manager instead.

2. THE THREE LEVELS OF AN AD
  Campaign   the goal. DAUR uses "Engagement" with WhatsApp as the conversion
             location (Click to WhatsApp), and later "Sales" for website orders.
  Ad set     who sees it, where, and the budget: Pakistan, men 20 to 45,
             Advantage+ placements.
  Ad         what they see: image or video, text, the "Send WhatsApp message"
             button.

3. CLICK TO WHATSAPP, IN ONE PARAGRAPH
The ad has a button that opens a WhatsApp chat with 0319 9628735 and a ready
first message. You answer like a shop assistant and close the order in the chat
(see "03 Selling on WhatsApp with COD.txt"). WhatsApp's own guide says these ads
can use the Engagement, Traffic or Sales objective, and that any offer promised
in the ad must be delivered quickly once the chat starts.

4. THE LEARNING PHASE (why you must not keep editing)
After you publish, Meta spends some days learning who responds. Industry
summaries of Meta's help pages say it usually needs about 50 optimisation
events in 7 days, and that big edits (targeting, creative, optimisation event,
or a budget change of more than about 20%) restart learning. So: publish, wait,
read on Day 04, change only on Day 08.

5. THE NUMBERS IN ADS MANAGER, IN PLAIN WORDS
  Amount spent      money used so far.
  Reach             how many different people saw the ad.
  Impressions       how many times it was shown (one person can see it twice).
  Frequency         impressions divided by reach. When it keeps rising and the
                    cost per chat rises with it, people are tired of the ad:
                    refresh creatives (rule of thumb, not a Meta rule).
  CPM               cost per 1,000 impressions. How expensive the audience is.
  CTR (link)        out of 100 people who saw it, how many clicked. Shows if the
                    creative and first line are interesting.
  Messaging conversations started
                    chats opened from the ad. The main result for Campaign 1.
  Cost per messaging conversation started
                    amount spent divided by chats. Watch this every few days.
  Purchases / ROAS  only for website Sales campaigns, once the Pixel is set up.
                    ROAS = sales value divided by ad spend.
Ads Manager does not know which chats became paid, delivered orders. Count that
yourself in DAUR Numbers.xlsx (Weekly sheet): chats, confirmed, delivered.

6. WHAT GOOD LOOKS LIKE
Nobody can tell you a correct cost per chat before your own ads run. Agency
blogs quote Pakistani ranges, but none publish audited data, so they are not
used in this plan. The real test is: ad spend divided by DELIVERED orders must
stay below the break even number in DAUR Numbers.xlsx.

7. PAYING FOR ADS FROM PAKISTAN
The payment methods Meta offers depend on the ad account's country and
currency, which cannot be changed after the ad account is created. Create the
ad account with country Pakistan and currency PKR unless your bank card only
works in USD. Use a debit or credit card that is enabled for international
online payments; ask your bank to enable it if a payment is declined. Taxes on
ad spend: Pakistan introduced a "Digital Presence Proceeds Levy" in 2025 for
foreign digital platforms; whether and how it appears on Meta invoices should be
confirmed with your bank statement or a tax adviser. Do not guess this.

8. RULES THAT GET ADS REJECTED (Meta Advertising Standards)
  * Before and after or "too good to be true" claims.
  * Personal attribute wording such as "Are you short?".
  * Prices or offers in the ad that differ from the store or chat.
  * Images that are mostly text can deliver less; keep text short.
