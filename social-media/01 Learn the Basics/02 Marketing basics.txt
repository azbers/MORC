DAUR SUPPLY  |  MARKETING BASICS FOR A NEW OWNER
Written 29 October 2026. Sources are listed at the end and in "08 Sources.txt".

1. WHAT MARKETING IS, FOR DAUR
Getting the right men in Pakistan to (1) notice DAUR, (2) trust it enough to
order with Cash on Delivery, (3) accept the parcel at the door, and (4) come back
or tell a friend. Every post, ad and message should push one of those four.

  Stage        What the buyer thinks            What DAUR gives them
  Notice       "What is this?"                  Reels, ads, a clear first line
  Trust        "Is this real? Will it fit?"     COD, 5 day size exchange, real
                                                photos, Fit notes, fast replies
  Order        "How do I buy?"                  WhatsApp chat, simple checkout
  Come back    "That was good."                 the box, the card, a follow up

2. ORGANIC VERSUS PAID
  Organic   posts, Reels and Stories that people see for free. Slow, but it
            builds the page that ads send people to.
  Paid      ads in Meta Ads Manager (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp) and later
            TikTok. Fast and measurable, but costs money each day.
You need both. An ad that sends people to an empty profile wastes money; a nice
profile with no ads grows slowly. The 30 day calendar in "04 Platforms" runs
both together.

3. WHERE YOUR BUYERS ARE (Pakistan, late 2025, DataReportal Digital 2026)
  TikTok      79.9 million users aged 18+, 71.2% of the adult audience male
  YouTube     54.3 million ad reach
  Facebook    52.9 million ad reach, 79.2% of the adult ad audience male
  Instagram   22.4 million ad reach, 63.0% male
  Messenger   15.5 million
DataReportal warns that ad reach is not the same as active users. Even so, it
says clearly: Facebook and TikTok reach the most Pakistani men. Instagram is
smaller but is where people check if a brand looks real. This is why the plan
is Meta first (Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp in one ad), TikTok second.

4. WHY SHORT VIDEO (REELS AND TIKTOK) MATTERS
  * Buffer analysed over 4 million Instagram posts (January 2022 to October
    2024): Reels got about 36% more reach than carousels and 125% more than
    single photo posts. Carousels got the most engagement (likes, comments).
  * Adam Mosseri (head of Instagram, January 2025) named watch time, likes per
    reach and sends per reach (people sharing the post in a DM) as the three
    most important signals, and said original content gets more distribution
    than reposts.
  * A Meta and Toluna study of about 100 short video ads found ads that
    showed the brand and the message in the first five seconds were 1.7 times
    more likely to drive strong purchase intent.
What this means for DAUR: Reels to reach new people, carousels to teach and get
saved, single images least. Film your own real pairs; never repost other
brands' videos. Show DAUR and the main point in the first seconds.

5. CONTENT PILLARS (what to post about, in rotation)
  Product     the real pairs: turns, close ups, on foot, colours
  Proof       checks, the box, Fit notes, customer unboxings (with permission)
  Help        sizes, care, how COD works, how the exchange works
  Story       Naya daur, the honest math, workshop to door, the charity pledge
  Offer       SUPPLYLINE, free delivery on two pairs (one saving per order)
Keep Offer posts to about one in five, or the page reads like an ad.

6. WHAT TO MEASURE ON ORGANIC POSTS
Instagram Professional dashboard shows, per post: reach, saves, shares, profile
visits, follows. For DAUR the useful ones are shares (sends) and profile visits,
because they mean someone is interested. Likes alone do not pay.

7. RULES THAT PROTECT THE BRAND
  * No fake reviews, no fake stock counts ("only 2 left") unless true, no fake
    countdowns. They break customer trust and Meta's advertising standards.
  * No claims you have not tested (waterproof, genuine leather on a model you
    have not confirmed, slip resistant).
  * Reply to every comment and DM within working hours; speed is trust.
