DAUR SUPPLY  |  SHORT VIDEO PLAYBOOK (REELS AND TIKTOK)
Written 29 October 2026.

WHY THE FIRST KIT WAS ONLY IMAGES
The real pairs had not arrived, and the store images are drawings. A video of
drawings, or stock footage of other brands' shoes, would show customers
something DAUR does not sell, and Instagram gives less reach to unoriginal
content. So the first kit had only designed images.

WHAT IS NOW IN THE CALENDAR
  Days 01 to 14  a Reel (MP4, 1080x1920, no sound) in every Instagram,
                 Facebook and TikTok folder. Each is a slow motion version of
                 that day's real designs: they can be posted now and need no
                 product footage. Add music inside each app.
  Days 15 to 30  filming briefs (Plan.txt) for product Reels: reveal, close
                 ups, unboxing, size guide, styling, COD, care, walk test,
                 customer videos. Film them when the pairs arrive.

WHY VIDEO IS THE MAIN GROWTH TOOL (sources at the end)
  * Reach: Buffer's analysis of over 4 million posts (2022 to 2024) found
    Instagram Reels reached 36% more people than carousels and 125% more than
    single images.
  * Signals: Instagram's head named watch time, likes per reach and sends per
    reach as the most important ranking signals (January 2025).
  * Ads: a Meta and Toluna study of short video ads found those that showed
    the brand and message in the first 5 seconds were 1.7 times more likely to
    drive strong purchase intent.
  * Engagement: carousels still earn the most likes and comments on
    Instagram in Buffer's data, so keep carousels for teaching.
  * Pakistan: TikTok has the largest adult reach (79.9 million, DataReportal).
So: Reels and TikTok for reach, carousels for trust, single images least.

THE DAUR REEL FORMULA
  0 to 2 s   the hook: the shoe already moving, or a bold line of text.
             No logo intro, no black screen.
  2 to 10 s  one idea only: one pair, one detail, one answer.
  last 2 s   the action: "Order on WhatsApp 0319 9628735" or "daursupply.com".
  Length     7 to 20 seconds for most; up to 30 for tutorials.
  Sound      from the app's own library (TikTok business accounts: Commercial
             Music Library only). Add on screen text too: many people watch
             without sound.
  Captions   first line answers "why watch": "How to find your size at home".

HOW TO FILM WELL ON A PHONE
  Window light, no direct sun. Clean lens. Vertical. Phone still on books or a
  tripod; move the shoe. Plain background (espresso cloth or wooden table).
  Film each shot three times. Keep text away from the top 250 px and bottom
  340 px of the frame.

WHAT TO AVOID
  * Reposting other brands' or influencers' videos (unoriginal, may be
    copyright infringement).
  * Watermarked TikTok downloads posted on Instagram: upload the clean file.
  * Claims you cannot prove on camera.

HOW TO USE REELS IN ADS
After a Reel gets good organic shares, use the same video as an ad creative in
the Click to WhatsApp campaign (Ads Manager > Ad > Use existing post), so its
likes and comments carry over.
