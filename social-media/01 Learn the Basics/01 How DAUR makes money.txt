DAUR SUPPLY  |  HOW THE BUSINESS MAKES MONEY
Written 29 October 2026 for an owner who is new to business. Read this first.

1. THE WHOLE BUSINESS IN ONE LINE
You buy finished pairs from Pakistani workshops, check them, re-lace and box
them as DAUR, and sell them online with Cash on Delivery (COD). You keep the
difference between what the customer pays and everything it cost to get the
pair to them.

2. THE FIVE NUMBERS THAT DECIDE IF YOU MAKE MONEY
Every pair has these. Write them for every model before you sell it.

  A  Selling price        what the customer pays (for example Rs. 4,500)
  B  Product cost         what you pay the workshop for the pair
  C  Kit cost             box, tissue, laces, tag, seal, card (see 05 Packaging)
  D  Delivery cost        courier charge, COD handling fee, plus the cost of
                          parcels that are refused and come back
  E  Ad cost per order    total ad spend divided by delivered orders

  Profit per pair  =  A  minus  B  minus  C  minus  D  minus  E

The file "DAUR Numbers.xlsx" in this folder does this sum for you. Change the
yellow cells only.

3. THE ESTIMATES YOU ALREADY HAVE (not quotes, not facts yet)
From the original project: one delivered pair costs about Rs. 431 to Rs. 542
to fulfil (courier, COD and refused parcels together), and ads stop being
profitable above roughly Rs. 1,390 per order. These were estimates. Replace
them with the written Leopards tariff as soon as you have it. Leopards Retail (branch, verbal, Oct 2026): up to Rs. 350 per parcel (about Rs. 500 Sindh and Balochistan), 4% FBR withheld from the COD amount, optional 2% insurance, payout about 10 days after delivery, no charge for refused parcels. See 02 Business and Meta Setup Couriers.txt.

4. WHY REFUSED PARCELS MATTER SO MUCH
With COD the customer pays only when the parcel arrives. If they refuse it, you
usually pay the courier for the trip there and the trip back, and you have paid
for the ad that brought the order. A refused parcel costs money and earns
nothing. This is why every order is confirmed on WhatsApp or by call before it
is booked (see "03 Selling on WhatsApp with COD.txt").

5. BREAK EVEN, IN PLAIN WORDS
Break even is the most you can spend on ads to get one delivered order and
still make zero profit. Anything below it is profit. The spreadsheet shows it as
"Most you can pay for one order". Watch this number every week in Ads Manager
(cost per purchase or cost per conversation, divided by how many chats become
delivered orders).

6. CASH FLOW: THE TRAP FOR NEW SELLERS
You pay the workshop and the ads now. The courier pays you the COD money later
(Leopards Retail pays about 10 days after delivery, as the branch told the owner; check the contract). Keep
enough cash for at least two weeks of stock, ads and packaging before launch.
Do not spend money you are still waiting to receive from the courier.

7. WHAT TO DO EACH WEEK (5 minutes)
  * Fill the "Weekly" sheet in DAUR Numbers.xlsx: ad spend, chats, orders,
    delivered, refused, returned for size, cash received.
  * Look at two numbers: delivered orders and profit after ads.
  * Send the sheet to Claude for the weekly review.
