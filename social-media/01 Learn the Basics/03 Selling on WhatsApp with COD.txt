DAUR SUPPLY  |  SELLING ON WHATSAPP WITH CASH ON DELIVERY
Written 29 October 2026.

Most DAUR orders will start as a WhatsApp chat (from a Click to WhatsApp ad, the
Instagram bio link or the website button). The chat is the shop counter.

1. THE ORDER FLOW, STEP BY STEP
  1. Chat starts. Greeting message goes out automatically (WhatsApp Business >
     Business tools > Greeting message; text in 04 Platforms/WhatsApp/Setup).
  2. Ask three things only: which pair, which colour, which EU size.
     If they are unsure of size, ask for foot length in cm (heel to longest
     toe) and match it to the chart.
  3. Send the price, delivery fee (Rs. 200 for one pair, free on two or more),
     and the delivery time (dispatch within 24 hours, 2 to 6 working days).
  4. Ask for: full name, full address with city, a second phone number if
     they have one. Or send the product link so they order on the website.
  5. Confirm in one message (template below) and ask them to reply "Confirm".
  6. Book the parcel in the courier app only after "Confirm".
  7. Send the tracking number the same day.
  8. Two days after delivery: thank you message and the Fit note link.

2. CONFIRMATION MESSAGE (paste as a quick reply /confirm)
  Assalam o Alaikum [Name], thank you for your order with DAUR Supply.
  Pair: [model], [colour], EU [size]
  Total to pay the rider: Rs. [amount] (Cash on Delivery)
  Address: [address], [city]
  Dispatch within 24 hours, delivery in 2 to 6 working days.
  Please reply "Confirm" so we can book your parcel.

3. WHY CONFIRMATION MATTERS
A refused COD parcel costs two courier trips and earns nothing (see
"01 How DAUR makes money.txt"). A clear confirmation with the exact amount
removes surprises at the door. If someone does not reply within a day, send one
polite reminder; if there is still no reply, do not ship.

4. SIGNS AN ORDER MAY BE REFUSED
  * No reply to the confirmation.
  * Address with no city or landmark.
  * Several pairs in several sizes "to try".
  * A number that does not answer a call.
For these, call before booking. It is fine to ask a customer to confirm by
voice.

5. QUICK REPLIES ALREADY WRITTEN
"04 Platforms/WhatsApp/Setup/Messages.txt" has the greeting, away message and
quick replies: /exchange (5 days), /defect, /wrongitem, /saving and more. Add
/confirm from section 2 above.

6. RESPONSE TIME
A buyer who asked about shoes will often buy from whoever answers first. Set an away message for hours you are at your day job
that says when you will reply, and keep that promise.

7. WHAT NOT TO DO
  * No advance payment requests: COD is the promise.
  * No pressure lines or invented scarcity.
  * Do not send marketing broadcasts to people who have not opted in. WhatsApp
    can restrict accounts that many people block or report.
