YOUR ORDER OF WORK (each step links to its file)
  This week, while the pairs are in transit
   1. Secure Gmail, then Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp
      (04 Platforms\<platform>\Setup).
   2. Meta Business Portfolio, ad account, second admin
      (02 Business and Meta Setup\02 Meta Business Portfolio setup.txt).
   3. Claim TikTok (04 Platforms\TikTok\Setup).
   4. Open a courier account and get written rates (02 ...\03 Couriers.txt).
   5. NTN and the trademark search (02 ...\01 Legal, tax and trademark.txt).
   6. Postiz one time setup (06 Postiz\00 READ ME Postiz.txt).
  When the pairs arrive
   7. Photograph and film them (DAUR Photo Guide, and the Plan.txt briefs for
      Days 15 to 30).
   8. Measure the largest pair; send 05 Packaging to the printer for samples.
   9. Add real products in Shopify (03 Shopify\Product listing template.txt).
  10. Finish 03 Shopify\Shopify upgrade and update plan.txt, section A.
  Launch
  11. Set the real Day 01 date:
        open a terminal in 04 Platforms and run
        node change-launch-date.mjs YYYY-MM-DD
  12. Remove the store password. Day 01 posts and the Day 01 ads go live.
  Every week
  13. Fill DAUR Numbers.xlsx (Weekly) and send it to Claude.
