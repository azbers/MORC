RuleDetail
Size exchange5 days from delivery. Unworn, original box, seal attached. Customer sends it back with the courier of their choice and pays the return courier; the new size is dispatched once it passes the check.
Defects30 days (stitching opens or sole separates in normal wear): replace or refund.
Wrong or damaged itemReport within 48 hours with photos; an unboxing video helps.
Sale pairsFinal for size. Defects within 30 days are still covered.
RefundsTo a bank account or mobile wallet when no replacement is available.
HolidaysOrders confirmed on a Sunday or holiday dispatch the next working day.

Never mention free returns. Shopify's default Refund, Shipping and Terms policies still say 5 business days and must be replaced with the DAUR texts.

10Operations

Order flow

  1. Order arrives
  2. Confirm with the buyer by call or WhatsApp
  3. Pack, relace, tag and seal
  4. Book in the courier app from the order page
  5. Rider collects, delivers and collects cash
  6. Courier pays out minus charges

Couriers

Leopards Retail (owner's branch visit, verbal): up to Rs. 350 per parcel, about Rs. 500 for Sindh and Balochistan, paid in advance; 4% FBR withheld from COD; optional 2% insurance; payout about 10 days after delivery; no return charge; 2 to 3 attempts; no opening before payment; handed in before 5 pm ships same day; no Shopify integration on Retail, and Retail cannot become Merchant. Volume discount 10% at 10 a day up to 40% at 100. Full notes and questions to ask: 03 Couriers.txt. PostEx and Trax remain backups.

WhatsApp automation

Watix chosen (USD 10 per 30 days). The number shows Ineligible while active in the Business app; retry with Coexistence after 7 or more days of real use.

11Open actions for the owner

  1. Publish the "5 day exchange (review)" theme after comparing it with the live theme.
  2. Paste the DAUR Refund, Shipping, Terms and Contact policies in Settings > Policies.
  3. Set return rules: 5 days, customer provides return shipping, Sale collection final sale.
  4. Turn on "Include tax in prices"; set a rate only if one really applies.
  5. Switch off local pickup.
  6. Paste the rebuilt notification emails and the Order Printer packing slip; send test emails.
  7. Set the 8 sample products to Draft; add real products with photos, prices, stock and materials.
  8. Remove the store password only when real products are ready, then set up Google Search Console.
  9. Fill Shopify Brand social links (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok when claimed).
  10. Paste the updated WhatsApp profile text and quick replies (5 day exchange, /defect, /wrongitem, /saving).
  11. Get the Leopards rates in writing, ask the head office what a Merchant account needs (Retail cannot be upgraded), then open the account.
  12. Create the Meta business portfolio and ad account (Beginner Guides, parts 2 and 4).
  13. Put photos and videos of the real pairs in Social Media\Product Photos as soon as they arrive.
  14. Trademark search for the DAUR logo; ask a mufti about Tirmidhi 1232; confirm 2026 as the founding year.
  15. Make sure every quality check promised on the site happens for every pair.
