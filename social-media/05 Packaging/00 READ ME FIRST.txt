Made 30 October 2026. The previous kit (version 2) is kept unchanged in

THE IDEA
The box looks and feels like the website: espresso pebble leather, brass,
hide cream. To keep costs low we do NOT print boxes. We buy PLAIN boxes
and stamp them ourselves. Only what adds value to the customer is kept.

WHAT IS IN THE KIT (and why each item earns its place)
  Box          Plain espresso or dark brown box with a leather grain
               (leatherette) paper finish. Stamped by us on the lid.
  Stamp 1      LID wordmark, 120 x 44 mm. The one brand moment on the box.
  Stamp 2      END size label, 80 x 30 mm. Write size, colour and number by
               hand when packing; saves picking errors and replaces a
               printed size sticker.
  Stamp 3      Small loop D, 25 x 25 mm. Optional, for the other end.
  Tissue       PLAIN cream tissue (no printing), closed with the sticker.
  Round sticker 50 mm. Closes the tissue; the one printed item inside.
  Manifest Card A6, front and back. Front: why DAUR exists. Back: the real
               terms (5 day size exchange, 30 day defects, 48 hours wrong or
               damaged), care, Fit notes, contacts. It answers questions
               before they become WhatsApp messages.
  Supply Unit seal 50 x 30 mm, front and back. Needed for size exchanges.
  Lace tag     Brass, laser engraved DAUR, 30 x 10 mm. Back is optional.
  Laces        Thick matte black woven cotton (spec sheet in 06 Laces).

FILES
  Every item has an SVG (editable, vector, all text converted to outlines)
  and a PDF at true size. Stamp files are black = ink. Give them to the
  stamp maker as they are; the maker mirrors the rubber himself (say so).
  00 Mockups has every box side, the open box, and every card front and back.

HOW TO STAMP (in house)
  1. Order Stamp 1 as a rubber or polymer stamp on a wooden block, NOT a
     self inking stamp: a block lets you rock it evenly on a 120 mm mark.
  2. Ink: a metallic gold PIGMENT ink pad that states it works on coated or
     glossy paper. Dye ink smears on leatherette. Test on one spare box first.
  3. Use a guide: a cardboard L square taped to the table, so every lid is
     stamped in the same place (centre of the lid).
  4. Let it dry 10 to 15 minutes before boxing; a hair dryer on low speeds
     it up.
  Upgrade later: a manual hot foil press with a brass die gives a true
  foil finish. International listings seen on 30 Oct 2026 ran from about
  USD 175 to USD 535 before shipping and import charges (zonesuntech.com,
  wutaleather.com). Only worth it at steady volume.

MONEY: WHAT TO GET QUOTED BEFORE BUYING
  I could not find reliable Pakistani prices online for plain shoe boxes or
  custom logo stamps, so nothing below is a guess. Get two written quotes
  for each and put them in DAUR Numbers.xlsx (sheet "Per pair"):
    a) Plain box, leatherette wrapped rigid, 330 x 205 x 130 mm outside,
       per piece at 50 and at 200 pieces (Lahore: Shah Alam Market and
       Kashmir Road paper and packaging markets; also packit.pk).
    b) Plain box, corrugated, dark brown or black, same size (cheaper
       fallback; the stamp still works).
    c) Rubber or polymer stamps 1, 2 and 3 (one time cost).
    d) Gold pigment ink pad (lasts many boxes).
    e) Round stickers, 50 mm, per 100.
    f) Manifest Cards, A6, 350 gsm or more, two sided, per 100.
    g) Seals and brass lace tags, per 100.
  The only local price found: a do it yourself self inking text stamp kit,
  Rs. 1,950 (stationerystation.pk, 30 Oct 2026). It suits Stamp 2 text,
  not the logo.
  Rule of thumb for the margin: total packaging per pair (a + share of c
  and d + e + f + seal + tag + laces + tissue) should stay a small, known
  number. Write the real figure in the sheet before setting prices.

SIZES
  Box outside 330 x 205 x 130 mm fits most men's pairs to EU 45; check with
  the largest real pair before buying in bulk, and ask the courier how the
  box is billed (volumetric weight).

TYPE AND COLOUR
  Wordmark and labels: Cinzel. Body text: Cormorant Garamond. Both are free
  for commercial use (SIL Open Font License, Google Fonts). Colours:
  Espresso #2A1C14, Hide #F5EEE4, Brass #B0843F, gold ink about #C29A55.
  Leather surfaces in the mockups use a CC0 (free) photographed leather
  texture from Poly Haven, recoloured.
