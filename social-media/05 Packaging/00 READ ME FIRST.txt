WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER
  01 Shoe Box            Outside artwork on the dieline, dieline only, foil mask
                         for the lid logo, optional inside lid print
  02 Tissue Paper        Seamless repeat tile (127 mm) and full sheet 20 x 30 in
  03 Laces               Specification sheet (no artwork is printed on laces)
  04 Lace Tag            Brass DAUR tag: 1 to 1 laser engraving files + drawing
  05 Supply Unit Seal    Zip tie seal tag: front and back 1 to 1 + drawing
  06 Manifest Card       A6 card front and back (PRINT files and GUIDE files)
  07 Stickers            Round tissue seal 2 in, box seal strip, size label
  08 Courier Flyer Bag   Front print for a black poly mailer
  09 Previews            PNG pictures for viewing on the phone only

EVERY DESIGN COMES IN TWO FORMATS
  .svg  Open in Adobe Illustrator, CorelDRAW, Inkscape or Figma. All text is
        already converted to shapes, so no fonts are needed.
  .pdf  Same design, for printers who ask for PDF.
  Groups named "...do-not-print" (cut, crease, bleed, safe zone, labels) are
  guides. The designer hides or deletes them before sending to print.

THREE THINGS BEFORE PRINTING IN VOLUME
  1. Put your biggest real pair (EU 45) in a sample box. The box size here is
     a Pakistani stock men's size (12.5 x 7.5 x 5 in) and is not yet tested
     with DAUR stock.
  2. Ask for a printed sample (proof) and approve it in hand. Screen colours
     are not print colours.
  3. Trademark: no search has been done for the DAUR logo yet. Do it before
     ordering boxes or tags in large numbers.
