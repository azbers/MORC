FILE TYPES
  .svg  Open in Adobe Illustrator, CorelDRAW, Inkscape or Figma. Text is already
        converted to shapes, so no fonts are needed.
  .pdf  Same design, for printers who ask for PDF.
  PRINT = goes to press.  GUIDE = shows cut and safe lines, for checking only.
  FOIL / SPOT UV / DEBOSS MASK = black shows where that finish goes.

THREE THINGS BEFORE PRINTING IN VOLUME
  1. Put your biggest real pair (EU 45) in a sample box of 12.5 x 7.5 x 5 in.
  2. Ask for a printed sample (proof) and approve it in hand. Screen colours
     are not print colours, and the mockups are renders, not photos.
  3. Trademark: no search has been done for the DAUR logo yet. Do it before
     ordering boxes or tags in large numbers.
