1. Start here

This folder already contains finished design files. A designer or vendor does not need to start from zero: they open the file for their item, check it against their machine, and print. Your job is to send the right folder to the right vendor, get a sample, and approve it.

  1. Measure first. Put your biggest real pair (EU 45) with tissue into a sample box of 12.5 x 7.5 x 5 in. If it does not close easily, tell the box maker the size you need; the design adapts.
  2. Get quotes from two or three vendors per item using the messages in section 9.
  3. Approve a physical sample before paying for the full batch. Colours on a phone screen never match print exactly.
  4. Trademark. No trademark search has been done for the DAUR logo. Run one before ordering boxes or tags in large quantity.

4. The shoe box

Style

The design uses a FEFCO 0427 style box. FEFCO is the European Federation of Corrugated Board Manufacturers, which gives standard codes to common box shapes, so any box maker understands the code. A 0427 is a one piece die cut box that ships flat, folds without glue or tape, and locks its lid with tabs; it is a standard choice for posting products (Kilby Packaging). Pakistani vendors describe the same thing as a "mailer box" or "ecommerce mailer box" (Imprint Packages, Megapack).

Size

Inside size 318 x 191 x 127 mm (12.5 x 7.5 x 5 in). This is the stock size Falcon Boxes lists for "men's shoes, adult sneakers". Using a stock size usually means the vendor already has the cutting die. It has not been tested with DAUR pairs, so test EU 45 first.

Size and delivery cost

Leopards Courier charges parcels larger than 12 x 12 x 12 cm by volumetric weight: length x breadth x height in cm, divided by 5000. For this box: 31.8 x 19.1 x 12.7 / 5000 = about 1.54 kg before board thickness, so expect to be billed at about 1.5 to 2 kg per pair even though a pair weighs less. A box even 2 cm smaller in each direction lowers this. Ask each courier which divisor they use; I verified it only for Leopards.

Material and print

  • Board: E flute corrugated (Imprint Packages lists E flute for shipping boxes). Ask for 3 ply.
  • Outside: printed solid black. For large black areas the common print recipe is C60 M40 Y40 K100 ("rich black"); small text should be plain K100 (4over4).
  • Finish: matte lamination to reduce scuffs (offered by Imprint Packages).
  • Lid logo: hot foil stamping in gold or brass tone. Foil is opaque and sits on top of the board, so it shows clearly on black; it needs a separate foil die, charged per colour and position (Print Peppermint). The file "FOIL MASK" shows exactly where.
  • Cheaper option: print the lid logo in brass coloured ink instead of foil (no foil die, less shine).

Artwork placement

Lid: stacked logo 74 mm wide and a thin keyline 10 mm inside the edges, both in brass foil, with "Men's footwear" and "Islamabad / Pakistan" in small cream type. Front: DAUR wordmark in cream and a clear area for the size label. Back: "Naya daur." and "Same local craft. Zero retail markup." Sides: a large DAUR wordmark in clear gloss varnish (spot UV) that shows only when light catches it, with daursupply.com on one side and @daursupply and WhatsApp on the other. Inside: espresso with "Wear the craft. Own the room." under the lid. Panels that look upside down in the flat file are correct; they read the right way up once folded.

The box files are split by finish, which is how printers work: 2 PRINT ink layer (CMYK), 3 FOIL MASK (where the foil goes), 4 SPOT UV MASK (where the gloss goes), 5 DIELINE, and 1 PREVIEW showing all of them together. Removing the spot UV and printing the logo in brass ink instead of foil are the two easiest ways to cut cost.

The dieline in this folder is a layout guide drawn to the exact size. Every box maker adjusts panels for their own board thickness, so ask them to send their own dieline for this size, then the designer moves the artwork onto it. Imprint Packages states that it draws dielines free from product dimensions.

5. The other items

Tissue paper

Cream tissue with a small D symbol and the words Naya daur and DAUR Supply in a diagonal repeat, printed in 1 colour espresso. noissue's specifications: 17 gsm is used for 1 colour designs with under 50% ink coverage; heavier 28 gsm is needed for full background or 2 colour printing; lines thinner than about 0.3 mm and text under about 5 pt should be avoided; vector files (AI or PDF) are required. This design has very low ink coverage and no lines thinner than 0.3 mm, so it fits 17 gsm. If cream tissue is not available, white tissue works with the same file. Sheet size 20 x 30 in is a standard listed size (Cubit Packaging, US). I could not verify a Pakistani vendor of printed 17 gsm tissue online; printed butter paper is available locally (Aprints, Lahore: sizes include 18 x 28 in, 100 sheets minimum, listed from Rs. 13 to Rs. 16 a sheet), and the same tile prints on it.

Laces

Matte black woven cotton, flat 6 to 8 mm. Pear Shoelace lists flat cotton laces at 6 mm wide in lengths from 50 cm to 160 cm. Length depends on the number of eyelet pairs; Slickies Laces' chart gives, for medium (sneaker) spacing, 100 cm for 5 pairs, 110 cm for 6 and 120 cm for 7, and for narrow (dress) spacing 80 cm for 5 or 6 pairs. Count the eyelet pairs on each real model and test one pair before ordering.

Brass lace tag (deubre)

A small metal plate threaded on the lace. Proposed size 30 x 12 mm, about 1 mm brass, 2.4 x 9.5 mm slots so a lace up to 9 mm passes through, DAUR wordmark laser engraved 17 mm wide. These sizes are my design proposal, not an industry standard; approve one sample. Custom engraved deubres are a known product (LaceSpace, Australia, engraves them individually in gold, silver, gunmetal and black). Placement: lowest eyelet pair, near the toe.

DAUR SUPPLY UNIT seal

A soft matte black tag with the D symbol pressed into it (deboss), on a one time locking strap through the top eyelet. Once cut, it cannot be refitted, which is why the size exchange requires it attached. Put a serial number on each seal and write it on the order (Shopify order note and the size label) so a returned pair can be matched. Custom embossed plastic tags and numbered pull tight security seals are both sold by overseas suppliers (Alibaba and others); I did not find a verified Pakistani maker online, so ask local garment accessory suppliers for a quote.

Manifest Card

A6 (105 x 148 mm), 350 gsm card (Imprint Packages lists 300 and 350 gsm card), matte lamination; ask whether soft touch lamination is available. Front: black, with the logo and a short rule in brass foil, and the DAUR story. Back: the size exchange (5 days, unworn, original box, seal attached, return courier paid by the customer, sale pairs final for size), defects (30 days), wrong or damaged item (48 hours with photos), care, Fit notes, the charity pledge and contacts. These match the current store policies; if a policy changes, the card must be updated before reprinting.

Stickers

Round 2 in tissue seal in black with brass foil text around the edge (Megapack lists 2 x 2 in among its sticker sizes; brass ink is the cheaper option), a 100 x 25 mm black box seal strip across the lid edge ("If this seal is broken, tell us within 48 hours"), and a cream 70 x 40 mm size label filled by pen with style, colour, EU size and seal number.

Courier flyer bag

The closed box measures roughly 324 x 197 x 133 mm outside. To wrap it, the bag needs about 360 mm width and 500 mm length plus a 50 mm adhesive flap. Confirm by trying the real box in a sample bag. Megapack sells courier flyer bags with a 1,000 piece minimum for printing.

6. Print terms in plain language

TermMeaning
DielineThe flat outline of a box: solid lines are cut, dashed lines are folded (creased). The artwork is placed on it.
Die / cutting dieThe metal blade tool made from the dieline that cuts the board. Stock sizes often already have one; a new size may need a new die.
BleedExtra artwork, usually 3 mm (1/8 in), past the cut line so no white edge appears if the cut moves slightly (4over4).
Safe zoneAn inner margin (at least 3 mm, safer 6 mm) where text and logos must stay so they are not cut off.
CMYKThe four ink colours used in normal printing (cyan, magenta, yellow, black). Screens use RGB, so colours shift when printed.
Pantone / spot colourA premixed ink matched from a printed swatch book, for an exact colour. Costs more than CMYK on small runs.
Rich blackBlack made with all four inks (for example C60 M40 Y40 K100) so large areas look deep, not grey (4over4).
Hot foil stampingA heated metal block presses metallic foil onto the board. Shiny and opaque; needs a separate black and white "mask" file (Print Peppermint).
Lamination (matte, gloss, soft touch)A thin film over the print. Matte reduces shine and scuffs; soft touch feels velvety.
Spot UVA clear shiny coating on selected areas only.
GSMGrams per square metre: paper weight. Tissue is about 17 to 28 gsm; card is 300 to 350 gsm.
Flute, E flute, 3 plyCorrugated board has a wavy middle layer (the flute). E flute is a thin flute used for retail and mailer boxes. 3 ply means two flat sheets with one wavy layer.
Vector file (SVG, AI, PDF, EPS)A file made of shapes, not pixels, so it stays sharp at any size. All files here are vector.
Outlined textText converted into shapes so the printer does not need the font. Done in every file here.
ProofA sample (digital or printed) you approve before the full run. Always ask for a printed proof for colour.
MOQMinimum order quantity.
Volumetric weightCouriers charge by size when a parcel is light but large: L x B x H in cm divided by 5000 (Leopards).

Ask the printer to match them on a printed proof, or to match Pantone swatches against a physical sample you approve. I did not assign Pantone codes because that requires a physical Pantone guide.

Fonts

Space Grotesk (headings), Inter (body), JetBrains Mono (labels). All are free Google Fonts. In these files the text is already outlined, so fonts are only needed to edit wording.

File rules used

  • Units are millimetres; files are at real size (1 to 1) unless the name says "SPEC". Files named PRINT are what goes to press; GUIDE files show cut and safe lines for checking only.
  • 3 mm bleed on all flat printed items; safe zone 5 mm (card), 2.5 mm (small stickers), 6 mm (box panels).
  • Groups named CUT, CREASE, BLEED, SAFE, LABELS, NOTES or GUIDES are not to be printed.
  • Foil, spot UV and deboss areas are supplied as separate 100% black masks in the same position as the ink file.
  • Laser engraving files: black fill = engrave; magenta 0.1 mm line = cut outline.
  • The PDFs were exported from a browser and are in RGB colour. Convert to CMYK (or the printer's profile) before final output.
  • Logos come from the official files in Daur Supply/Logos/SVG; do not redraw or stretch them.

8. Packing steps (for whoever packs orders)

  1. Check the pair against the order: style, colour, EU size.
  2. Swap factory laces for DAUR black laces. Brass tag on the lowest eyelet pair, wordmark up.
  3. Fit the DAUR SUPPLY UNIT seal through the top eyelet of the right shoe. Write the seal number on the size label and in the Shopify order note.
  4. Line the box with one tissue sheet, place the pair, fold the tissue over, close with the round sticker.
  5. Manifest Card on top of the tissue, front facing up.
  6. Size label on the front of the box in the marked area. Close the lid, put the seal strip across the lid edge.
  7. Put the box in the flyer bag with the packing slip, seal it, stick the courier label on the bag.
