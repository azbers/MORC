DAUR SUPPLY  |  LEGAL, TAX AND TRADEMARK BASICS (PAKISTAN)
Written 29 October 2026. This is general information gathered from public
sources, not legal or tax advice. Where sources disagree, it says so. Confirm
with FBR, IPO Pakistan or a registered tax practitioner before acting.

1. TRADING AS A SOLE PROPRIETOR
A sole proprietorship is the simplest form: you are the business. No SECP
company registration is needed for it. You can form a private limited company
with SECP later if the business grows or takes partners.

2. NTN (NATIONAL TAX NUMBER) WITH FBR
  Where     FBR IRIS portal, https://iris.fbr.gov.pk
  How       create an IRIS account with your CNIC, mobile number and email,
            then file the registration form and add the business details
            (business name "DAUR Supply", nature of business, address).
  Documents CNIC, and a utility bill for the address, according to the guides.
  Cost      guides report no government fee for registration.
  Conflict  some guides say an individual's CNIC already serves as the NTN;
            others say a sole proprietor should register the business in IRIS.
            Ask FBR (helpline 051 111 772 772) or a tax practitioner which
            applies to you.
Why bother: a bank business account, courier merchant accounts, and later
payment gateways often ask for an NTN, and filing returns as a filer usually
means lower withholding tax on banking and other transactions.

3. SALES TAX
Sales tax registration (STRN) has thresholds and rules for retailers that
change with each budget. Ask a tax practitioner whether DAUR must register at
its current size. Until then, the store setting "Include tax in prices" stays
on and no tax rate is added (owner rule in the project memory).

4. BUSINESS BANK ACCOUNT
Open a separate account for DAUR, even a personal account used only for the
business. Courier COD payouts, Meta ad payments and workshop payments all go
through it. This makes the weekly numbers and any tax filing simple.

5. TRADEMARK FOR THE NAME AND LOGO
  Office    Intellectual Property Organization of Pakistan (IPO Pakistan),
            online filing at https://apply.ipo.gov.pk (according to guides).
  Class     Class 25 covers footwear and clothing. Class 35 covers retail and
            online store services. Pakistan accepts one class per application,
            so each class is a separate filing.
  Fees      third party guides from 2025 quote about Rs. 3,000 per class for
            filing, and total official fees of roughly Rs. 13,000 to 15,000 for
            one class without opposition. These are not official figures; check
            the IPO fee schedule before paying.
  Time      guides report 12 to 18 months from filing to certificate.
  Before printing boxes in volume: search the IPO database and WIPO's Global
  Brand Database (https://branddb.wipo.int) for DAUR in Class 25 and for
  similar D logos. A known clash to look at: daur.shop, a Karachi watch
  retailer. A trademark agent can do a proper search for a fee.

6. WHAT IS ALREADY SAFE TO DO
Selling under the DAUR Supply name while the application is pending is common
practice, but a registered earlier mark held by someone else could force a
rename. That is the reason to search before a large packaging print run.
