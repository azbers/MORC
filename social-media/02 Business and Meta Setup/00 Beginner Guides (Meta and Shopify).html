Part 1

The words, in plain language

WordWhat it means for DAUR
Business portfolioThe container that owns the DAUR Facebook Page, Instagram, WhatsApp number, ad account and Pixel. Meta used to call it "Business Manager". It is what keeps the business safe if one personal profile gets locked.
Meta Business SuiteThe free website and phone app (business.facebook.com) where you post, schedule, answer messages, read insights and make simple ads. It needs a business portfolio.
Ads ManagerThe full advertising tool. Use it for real campaigns: Click to WhatsApp, website sales, retargeting. Business Suite only makes quick ads and boosts.
Ad accountThe wallet and history of your ads: currency, time zone, payment card, every campaign. Currency and time zone cannot be changed after it is created.
Pixel / datasetA tracking code from Meta on daursupply.com that tells Meta who viewed, added to cart and bought, so ads can find similar buyers. Shopify's Facebook and Instagram app installs it for you.
BoostPaying to show an existing post to more people. Simple, but with far fewer controls than Ads Manager.
Part 2

Business portfolio (formerly Business Manager)

Do this once, before any ad spend.

Create it

  1. Go to business.facebook.com and log in with your own personal Facebook profile (the one that is admin of the DAUR Supply Page).
  2. Click the drop down menu below Home in the top left, then Create a business portfolio.
  3. Name it DAUR Supply. Meta says the name should match your public business name and cannot contain special characters.
  4. Enter your first name, surname and business email (daursupply@gmail.com). Meta uses it to contact you; customers do not see it.
  5. Click Create, then open the confirmation email Meta sends and confirm the address.
Meta's own limits: one person can create at most two business portfolios, and each asset (Page, Instagram, ad account, Pixel) can belong to only one portfolio. Meta also says a new portfolio or ad account may not have every advertising feature at first.

Add the DAUR assets

  1. Facebook Page: Settings, Accounts, Pages, + Add, Add an existing Facebook Page, type "DAUR Supply", tick the terms, Add Page. Adding the Page to the portfolio means the business owns it.
  2. Instagram @daursupply: it must be a professional account (Instagram, Settings, Account type and tools). In Settings, add the Instagram account and log in to it when asked. Meta notes that adding Instagram to the portfolio does not by itself link it to the Page; link them as well (Page settings, Linked accounts, Instagram).
  3. WhatsApp: link the WhatsApp Business app number 0319 9628735 from the app (Settings, Business tools, Facebook and Instagram). Do not move the number to the WhatsApp API: the Watix plan waits for Coexistence.

Add a second person

  1. Settings, People, Invite people, enter the email used on their Facebook account.
  2. Give portfolio permissions and pick assets. Meta advises giving only the access people need. They must accept the invitation; Meta says features can take a few days to appear for them.
  3. Turn on two factor authentication for everyone (Security Centre).
If you cannot see People, you do not have full control of the portfolio. If inviting fails, Meta's first fix is to confirm your email under Settings, Business info.

Part 3

Meta Business Suite, day to day

Meta describes Business Suite as free on desktop and in the phone app, and it needs the business portfolio from Part 2.

TabWhat you do there
HomeTo do list, recent and scheduled posts.
Content and PlannerCreate, schedule and see posts, Stories and Reels for Facebook and Instagram in a weekly or monthly calendar. This is the free alternative to Postiz for days the PC will be off, because it runs on Meta's servers.
InboxOne inbox for Facebook Messenger, Instagram DMs, comments and a linked WhatsApp Business account. Set automations (instant reply, frequently asked questions) with the texts in 04 Platforms\WhatsApp\Setup.
InsightsReach, activity and audience. Read it weekly, not daily.
AdsQuick ads and boosts only. Meta itself points to Ads Manager for detailed campaigns with advanced targeting.
All toolsLinks to Ads Manager, Events Manager (Pixel), Billing and payments, Page settings.

Schedule a post (desktop)

  1. Planner or Content, Create post. Tick both Facebook and Instagram.
  2. Add the image or video from the day's folder in 04 Platforms and paste Caption.txt. Instagram posts must include media.
  3. Choose Schedule, pick the date and time from the folder name, save. Check it appears in the Planner.

Part 4

Ad accounts and Ads Manager

Create the DAUR ad account

  1. Business Suite, Settings, Accounts, Ad accounts, Add, Create a new ad account. (Meta lists three ways: create new, add an existing one you own, or request access to someone else's.)
  2. Name: DAUR Supply Ads. Time zone: Asia/Karachi. Currency: PKR if your card is charged in PKR. These two cannot be changed later; a wrong choice means a new ad account.
  3. Assign yourself full control, then open Billing and payments and add a payment method.
Limits that surprise beginners. Meta states that every advertiser starts with an ad account limit of one until a confirmed payment is made, and that a closed or long unused ad account still counts toward the limit. So create one account carefully and keep using it. See your limit under Settings, Business info.

Paying for ads from Pakistan

Meta's accepted payment page lists, for Pakistan, Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards, and PayPal. Before adding a card, ask your bank to enable international online and recurring payments on it, or the first charge fails and delivery stops. Meta's page on Pakistan is old, so check the list shown in your own Billing screen.

Your bank may add foreign transaction charges or taxes on top of what Meta bills, and the rates differ by bank; ask your bank in writing. Write the real billed amounts into DAUR Numbers.xlsx each week rather than relying on the budget figure in Ads Manager.

Business Suite ads or Ads Manager?

UseWhen
Boost in Business SuiteRarely. Only to push a post that is already doing well organically, for a small fixed amount.
Ads ManagerEvery real campaign in 04 Platforms\Meta Ads: Click to WhatsApp, website sales after the Pixel works, retargeting. It shows full results and lets you edit, pause and compare ads.

Ads Manager in five words: campaign, ad set, ad

  • Campaign: the goal (for DAUR month one: Engagement with messaging to WhatsApp, as set out in the Meta Ads Day 01 folder).
  • Ad set: who sees it, where, and the daily budget.
  • Ad: the image or video, text and button.

Every new ad is reviewed by Meta before it runs. Follow Meta's Advertising Standards: no before and after claims, no fake scarcity, real prices only.

Troubleshooting

  • "You've reached your ad account maximum": the limit above. Use the existing account.
  • Failed payment: fix the card in Billing and payments; ads stop until paid.
  • Disabled or restricted account: use Business Support Home in All tools to request a review. Never open a second profile to get around it; that breaks Meta's rules.

Part 5

Shopify upkeep for daursupply.com

Facts about this store that change the routine

  • The DAUR theme is a custom uploaded theme. Shopify states that uploaded themes do not receive the standard theme updates, so it never changes on its own. Every change comes from Claude as a new unpublished theme that you preview and publish.
  • Before publishing any new theme: keep the old one (do not delete it), open Preview, test a product page, the cart and checkout on your phone, then publish. If something breaks, publish the old one again; it takes one click.
  • Payment is Cash on Delivery only. Shopify charges third party transaction fees on transactions through third party and alternate payment gateways; check Settings, Billing for anything charged on your plan.

Before launch (once)

Follow 03 Shopify\Shopify upgrade and update plan.txt, section A. The biggest items: set the 8 sample products to Draft, add the real pairs with real photos, prices, stock and Cost per item, paste the policies, and remove the password only when all of that is done.

Adding a product properly

  1. Products, Add product. Title in the pattern "DAUR [Name], Men's [type]".
  2. Photos from the real pair only, in the order of the Photo Guide.
  3. Options named exactly Color and Size ("EU 42").
  4. Price, compare at price only if it was really sold at that price, Cost per item (what you paid the vendor) so Shopify reports true profit.
  5. Stock per size; status Active only when the pair is physically with you.

Apps

  • Every app is a monthly cost or a speed cost. Once a month, open Settings, Apps and remove anything not used in the last 30 days.
  • After installing an app, open the store on your phone and check speed and layout.
