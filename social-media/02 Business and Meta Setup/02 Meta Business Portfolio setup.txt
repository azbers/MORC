DAUR SUPPLY  |  META BUSINESS PORTFOLIO: FULL SETUP, IN ORDER
Written 29 October 2026. Meta renames menus often; if a name below differs on
your screen, look for the closest match or search inside Settings.

WHY THIS MATTERS
The Business Portfolio (business.facebook.com) is the one place that owns the
Facebook Page, Instagram, WhatsApp number, ad account and Pixel. If your
personal Facebook is ever locked, a second admin in the portfolio can still run
the business. Do this before any ad spend.

STEP 1  SECURE YOUR PERSONAL FACEBOOK FIRST
  [ ] Two factor authentication on (Settings > Accounts Center > Password and
      security > Two factor authentication). Use an authenticator app.
  [ ] Real name and real profile photo: Meta restricts accounts that look fake.

STEP 2  CREATE THE BUSINESS PORTFOLIO
  [ ] Go to business.facebook.com > Create a business portfolio.
  [ ] Name: DAUR Supply. Your name. Business email: daursupply@gmail.com.
  [ ] Business details: Islamabad, Pakistan; website https://daursupply.com.

STEP 3  ADD THE ASSETS (Settings > Accounts)
  [ ] Pages > Add > the DAUR Supply Page (you are already its admin).
  [ ] Instagram accounts > Connect > @daursupply (it must be a Professional
      account: Instagram > Settings > Account type and tools).
  [ ] WhatsApp accounts > add the Business app number 0319 9628735, or link it
      from the WhatsApp Business app: Settings > Business tools > Facebook &
      Instagram. Do not move the number to the WhatsApp API now; the Watix
      plan waits for Coexistence (see project memory section 9).

STEP 4  CREATE THE AD ACCOUNT (Settings > Accounts > Ad accounts > Add > Create)
  [ ] Name: DAUR Supply Ads.
  [ ] Time zone: Asia/Karachi. Currency: PKR, unless your card only pays in USD.
      Country and currency cannot be changed later.
  [ ] Add a payment method in Billing and payments. Turn on your card's
      international online payments with your bank first.

STEP 5  PIXEL AND CONVERSIONS API THROUGH SHOPIFY (Data sources > Datasets)
  [ ] Easiest route: in Shopify, install the "Facebook & Instagram" app by
      Meta, connect the DAUR Supply portfolio, Page, Instagram, ad account,
      and create or pick the Pixel inside the app.
  [ ] Set Customer data sharing to Maximum. Shopify's help explains: Standard
      is the Pixel only (ad blockers can stop it); Enhanced and Maximum add
      the Conversions API, which sends purchase events server to server.
  [ ] Check in Events Manager > Test events that both browser and server
      events arrive after a test order. A Shopify community thread reports
      cases where server events did not arrive; if so, contact Meta support.

STEP 6  VERIFY THE DOMAIN (Brand safety and suitability > Domains)
  [ ] Add daursupply.com. Choose DNS TXT verification and add the TXT record
      in Shopify > Settings > Domains > daursupply.com > Manage > DNS settings.
  Verifying shows Meta you own the website the ads link to.

STEP 7  PEOPLE AND SAFETY
  [ ] Settings > Users > People: add one trusted second admin (a family member
      with a real, secured Facebook profile). Write their name in the
      Accounts Register.
  [ ] Security Center: require two factor authentication for everyone.
  [ ] Business verification (Security Center > Start verification) asks for
      official business documents. It is not needed to start running ads; it
      becomes useful for the WhatsApp API and higher limits. Do it once the
      NTN or a business bank statement in the DAUR name exists.

STEP 8  INBOX AND AUTOMATIC REPLIES
  [ ] Meta Business Suite > Inbox shows Facebook, Instagram and (if linked)
      WhatsApp messages in one place, also in the Meta Business Suite phone app.
  [ ] Inbox > Automations: set Instant reply and Frequently asked questions
      using the texts from 04 Platforms/WhatsApp/Setup/Messages.txt.

STEP 9  WRITE IT DOWN
  [ ] Fill "Accounts Register.txt" in this folder: portfolio ID, ad account ID,
      Pixel ID, second admin, date done. Never write passwords there.
