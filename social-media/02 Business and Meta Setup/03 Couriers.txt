LEOPARDS: WHAT THE BRANCH SAID (owner's visit, October 2026)
  Account types   Two: Retail (open at any nearby branch) and Merchant
                  (open at the head office or area office). A Retail account
                  cannot be upgraded to Merchant later. All answers below are
                  for Retail.
  To open         Basic business, personal and bank details.
  Delivery charge At most Rs. 350 per parcel across Pakistan; up to about
                  Rs. 500 for Sindh and Balochistan. Paid in advance by DAUR.
  Volume discount Quoted as Rs. 350, then 10% off at 10 parcels a day, 20% at
                  25, 30% at 50, 40% at 100.
  Tax             4% FBR withholding is deducted from the COD amount before
                  payout. It cannot be avoided by the merchant.
                  (Example: on a Rs. 4,000 COD parcel, Rs. 160 is withheld.)
  Insurance       Optional, 2% of the pair's price; covers loss or missing
                  parcels. (Rs. 80 on a Rs. 4,000 pair.)
  Payout          Into the bank account about 10 days after delivery.
  Returns         No charge for a returned (refused) parcel on Retail.
  Attempts        2 to 3 delivery attempts; the rider calls the customer
                  before delivery; the customer gets delivery and tracking
                  messages.
  Open parcel     Not allowed: the customer cannot open the parcel before
                  paying.
  Speed           2 to 3 days across Pakistan, 3 to 4 days for Sindh and
                  Balochistan. Parcels handed in before 5 pm ship the same day.
  Pickup          No pickup charge stated, or DAUR drops parcels at the shop.
  Paperwork       No airway bill printing needed: Leopards provides the
                  packing wrapper and DAUR writes on it. No packaging rules
                  other than that the box must fit inside the wrapper.
  Shopify         No Shopify integration on Retail; Leopards provides its own
                  parcel management portal. Orders are booked by hand.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR DAUR (plain arithmetic, no estimates added)
  Per Rs. 4,000 COD order: Rs. 350 delivery + Rs. 160 FBR withholding
  + Rs. 80 insurance if taken = up to Rs. 590 before packaging and ads.
  The website charges the customer Rs. 200 for one pair and nothing on two
  pairs, so DAUR carries the rest. The full cost model belongs to the
  costed plan thread and to DAUR Numbers.xlsx.
  Cash flow: money arrives about 10 days after delivery, so stock for the
  first two weeks must be paid from savings.
  Retail cannot become Merchant. If you expect to need Shopify integration
  or Merchant terms later, ask the head office what Merchant requires before
  opening Retail.
  The website promises delivery in 2 to 6 working days; Leopards' 2 to 4
  days fits inside that, so no wording change is needed.
