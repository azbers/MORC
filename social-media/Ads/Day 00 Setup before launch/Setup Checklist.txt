       personal profile that owns the DAUR Supply Page.
[ ] 2. Business settings > Accounts > Ad accounts > Add > Create a new ad
       account. Name "DAUR Supply". Time zone Asia/Karachi. Choose the
       currency carefully; it cannot be changed later (a new ad account
       would be needed).
[ ] 3. Billing: add a payment method (card that allows online international
       payments, or the options Meta shows for Pakistan).
[ ] 4. Link WhatsApp: Page Settings > Linked accounts > WhatsApp > 0319 9628735.
       Link Instagram @daursupply in the same place.
[ ] 5. Shopify: install the free "Facebook & Instagram" sales channel app by
       Meta and connect the same business, Page, ad account and Pixel. Set
       data sharing to the highest level offered (Conversions API).
[ ] 6. Business settings > Brand safety > Domains: add daursupply.com and
       verify it (the Shopify app can do this, or use the meta tag method).
[ ] 7. Audiences (Ads Manager > Audiences > Create audience > Custom audience):
       a. "IG engaged 30d"   Instagram account, everyone who engaged, 30 days
       b. "FB engaged 30d"   Facebook Page, everyone who engaged, 30 days
       c. "Site visitors 30d" Website, all visitors, 30 days (after step 5)
       d. "Buyers 30d"       Website, Purchase, 30 days (used to exclude)
       e. "WA chats 30d"     Messaging, people who messaged the Page or
                             WhatsApp, 30 days (if offered)
[ ] 8. WhatsApp Business app: greeting message, away message and quick
       replies from "Social Media/WhatsApp/Messages.txt" are in place, so
       every ad chat gets an instant answer.
[ ] 9. Two factor authentication on the profile that owns the business.

Meta changes labels often. If a menu name differs, use the search box in
