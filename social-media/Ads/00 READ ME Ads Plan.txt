WHY THIS PLAN  (sources)
  * Facebook has the second largest ad reach in Pakistan (52.9 million) and
    its adult ad audience is 79.2% male; Instagram reaches 22.4 million, 63.0%
    male. DataReportal, Digital 2026: Pakistan (figures from late 2025).
    https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-pakistan
  * Click to WhatsApp ads can be built with the Engagement, Traffic and Sales
    objectives in Ads Manager; deliver any promised offer quickly once the
    chat starts. WhatsApp Business blog.
    https://whatsappbusiness.com/blog/click-to-whatsapp-ad-guide/
  * A Meta and Toluna study of about 100 short video ads found ads that set
    out brand and message in the first five seconds were 1.7 times more
    likely to drive strong purchase intent. Every DAUR creative shows the
    message and the D mark at once for this reason.
    https://tolunacorporate.com/what-makes-short-form-video-ads-work-meta-toluna-study/
  * The learning phase usually needs about 50 optimisation events in 7 days,
    and edits to targeting, creative, optimisation event or a budget change
    above about 20% restart it. This is why the plan says do not edit before
    Day 08 and raise budgets in steps of 20% at most. (Industry summary of
    Meta's help pages.)
    https://lebesgue.io/facebook-ads/facebook-ads-learning-phase-what-you-need-to-know-2024-update
  * Product photos: these creatives are typographic because real product
    photos do not exist yet. As soon as real photos arrive, send them to
    Claude; product creatives will be added to Day 08 and Day 15.
