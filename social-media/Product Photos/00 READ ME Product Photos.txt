DAUR SUPPLY  |  PRODUCT PHOTOS: FROM REAL PAIRS TO READY POSTS
Made 30 October 2026.

WHY THIS FOLDER EXISTS
Posts with only text do not show what people are buying. Every product
post and ad from now on is built from photos and videos of the real pairs.
No drawn or sample shoes are used, because showing a pair DAUR does not
sell would break customer trust (and Meta's ad rules on misleading ads).

WHAT TO DO WHEN THE PAIRS ARRIVE
1. Photograph each colour with the DAUR Photo Guide (8 turning shots, top,
   sole, close up, inside, pair, on foot). Daylight by a window, plain wall
   or the DAUR box behind it. Clean the pair first.
2. Copy the best photos into this folder. Name them simply, for example
   "Moc Runner Black 01.jpg".
3. Open products.csv (Excel or Notepad) and add one line per photo:
     photo         the file name, exactly
     product       e.g. DAUR Moc-Runner v1
     colour        e.g. Triple Black
     price_rs      the real selling price, numbers only (leave empty to
                   show no price)
     sizes         e.g. EU 40 to 45 (only sizes really in stock)
     line          optional short line; default is
                   "Same local craft. Zero retail markup."
4. Double click is not enough for Python files on every PC, so open a
   terminal in the _builder folder and run:
     python build_posts.py
5. Finished images appear in "_Ready posts\<product> <colour>\":
     Feed post 1080x1350, Story 1080x1920 (organic posting)
     Ad feed 1080x1350, Ad story 1080x1920 (with the WhatsApp and COD button)
   Copy them into the day folders in 04 Platforms, or tell Claude and it will.

VIDEOS
Put phone videos here too (vertical, 9:16). Reels are built with the shot
lists and scripts from the product visuals thread. Short video is the
format Instagram and TikTok push hardest to new viewers; see
01 Learn the Basics\07 Short video playbook.txt.

RULES
* Only real photos of pairs you physically have.
* Price on the image must equal the price in Shopify.
* No logos of other brands in the shot (supplier tags, boxes, insoles).
