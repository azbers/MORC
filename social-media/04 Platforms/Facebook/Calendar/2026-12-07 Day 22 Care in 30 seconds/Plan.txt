HOW TO FILM (phone, no extra gear)
  Vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps. Clean the lens first.
  Daylight from a window, no direct sun, no overhead tube light.
  Plain background: the espresso cloth, a wooden table or a clean wall.
  Hold the phone on a stack of books or a cheap tripod; move the shoe, not the phone.
  Record 2 to 3 times as much as you need, then cut to the length below.
  Text on screen: keep it inside the middle area, about 250 px clear at the top
  and 340 px clear at the bottom, because the app covers those parts.
  Music: add it inside Instagram or TikTok from their own library, so it is licensed
  for that app. Do not add copyrighted songs in an editor.
  Edit in CapCut or Instagram Edits (both free) or straight in the app.
