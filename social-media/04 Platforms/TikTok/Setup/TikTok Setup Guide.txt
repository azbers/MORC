DAUR SUPPLY  |  TIKTOK SETUP
Written 29 October 2026. Status: handle not claimed yet (Accounts Register).

WHY TIKTOK
DataReportal (Digital 2026: Pakistan, late 2025 data) counts 79.9 million
TikTok users aged 18 and over in Pakistan, 71.2% of the adult audience male:
the largest reach of any platform for DAUR's buyers. Organic TikTok can reach
non followers from the first video, which suits a new brand.

STEP 1  CLAIM THE HANDLE (do it now, even before posting)
  [ ] Install TikTok on the phone. Sign up with daursupply@gmail.com.
  [ ] Username: daursupply (if taken, daur.supply or daursupplypk). Write the
      result in 02 Business and Meta Setup/Accounts Register.txt.
  [ ] Name: DAUR Supply. Photo: Profile Picture (espresso) from
      04 Platforms/Instagram/Setup.
  [ ] Turn on two step verification (Settings and privacy > Security).

STEP 2  SWITCH TO A BUSINESS ACCOUNT
  [ ] Settings and privacy > Account > Switch to Business Account. Category:
      Shopping & Retail (or the closest option shown).
  [ ] Bio (80 characters max):
        Men's leather shoes and sneakers. Zero retail markup. COD Pakistan.
  [ ] Website link: https://daursupply.com (TikTok may show the link field only
      after certain conditions; add it when it appears).
  Business accounts can use only the Commercial Music Library for sounds.

STEP 3  CONNECT SHOPIFY (optional, month two)
  The "TikTok" app in the Shopify App Store connects the store and the TikTok
  Pixel for ads. Check in the app whether TikTok Shop and catalogue features
  are available for Pakistan before relying on them.

STEP 4  POSTING
  Days 01 to 14: TikTok\Calendar\<date>\Reel 1080x1920.mp4 and Caption.txt.
  Days 15 to 30: filming briefs (Plan.txt), same as the Instagram Reels.
  Post the same Reel on TikTok and Instagram, but download nothing from one app
  to post on the other with its watermark: upload the clean MP4 from the folder.

STEP 5  TIKTOK ADS (month two, per the Ads plan)
  TikTok Ads Manager is available in Pakistan as a self serve tool. TikTok's own
  help centre sets minimum budgets of more than USD 50 a day per campaign and
  more than USD 20 a day per ad group. That is more than the month one Meta
  budget per day, which is why TikTok ads wait for month two.
