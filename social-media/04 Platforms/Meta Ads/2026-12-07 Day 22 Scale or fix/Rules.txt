DAY 22  |  SCALE OR FIX

Look at the last 7 days only.

IF orders from ad chats are profitable (the original plan estimated ads stop
being profitable above roughly Rs. 1,390 ad cost per order; this is an
estimate, recheck it with real courier rates and product costs):
  Raise the budget of the better campaign by 20% at most. Wait 3 to 4 days
  before the next raise.

IF chats are coming but few people order:
  The problem is after the click. Check reply speed, the answers given, the
  product photos and sizes in stock. Do not raise the budget.

IF few chats are coming (cost per conversation rising week on week):
  Check Frequency. Above about 3 in 7 days, people are tired of the ads: add
  new creatives (ask Claude, ideally with real product photos and short
  videos) rather than changing targeting.

Never change more than one thing at a time.
