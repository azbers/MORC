DAUR SUPPLY  |  SHOPIFY: WHAT TO UPDATE, IN ORDER
Written 29 October 2026 from the live store state in the project memory.
Store: ppiveu-zp.myshopify.com, domain daursupply.com, Basic plan, PKR,
password protected. Claude can create products, collections, discounts, pages
and new (unpublished) themes through the connector; it cannot publish themes,
edit policies, checkout, taxes or Brand settings. Those steps are marked OWNER.

A. BEFORE LAUNCH (must be done)
  [ ] OWNER  Publish theme "DAUR Supply 5 day exchange (review)" after
             comparing it with the live theme (Online Store > Themes > Preview).
  [ ] OWNER  Settings > Policies: paste Refund, Shipping, Terms of Service and
             Contact texts (the DAUR versions, 5 day exchange).
  [ ] OWNER  Settings > Returns: 5 days, customer provides return shipping,
             Sale collection final sale, 0% restocking fee.
  [ ] OWNER  Settings > Taxes and duties: "Include tax in prices" on; no rate
             unless one really applies.
  [ ] OWNER  Shipping and delivery: switch off local pickup.
  [ ] OWNER  Settings > Notifications: paste each rebuilt DAUR email, send tests.
  [ ] OWNER  Order Printer: paste the DAUR packing slip; print a test.
  [ ] Set the 8 sample products to Draft. Add the real products (template in
      "Product listing template.txt"): real photos, price, stock per size,
      Cost per item, materials, options named Color and Size ("EU 42").
  [ ] Courier: open an account, connect it (Universal Courier Pakistan app or
      the courier's own app), book one test parcel.
  [ ] OWNER  Remove the store password (Online Store > Preferences) only when
      all of the above is done.

B. CONNECT SALES AND TRACKING (launch week)
  [ ] Install "Facebook & Instagram" by Meta; connect the Business Portfolio;
      data sharing Maximum (see 02 Business and Meta Setup, Step 5).
  [ ] Shop tab on Instagram and Facebook: available through that app if Meta
      approves the store for commerce in your country. Meta's commerce
      eligibility differs by country; the app will show whether it is
      available for Pakistan. Do not plan around it until it shows.
  [ ] Brand social links: Instagram and Facebook (Settings > Brand), TikTok
      when claimed.
  [ ] Google Search Console: verify daursupply.com and submit
      https://daursupply.com/sitemap.xml (after the password is removed).
  [ ] Note from research: Google Merchant Center free listings and Shopping
      ads do not support Pakistan, and Google Business Profile is not for an
      online only store. Skip both.

C. ORDER AUTOMATION (after 7 or more days of real WhatsApp use)
  [ ] Retry Watix with WhatsApp Coexistence for COD confirmation, retries and
      tracking messages. Ask Watix in writing if Coexistence works for
      Pakistani numbers.

D. UPGRADES WORTH DOING (in this order, each after the last is working)
  1. "Order on WhatsApp" and "Order now, pay on delivery" buttons on product
     pages (Claude can build this as a new review theme).
  2. Size chart on every product page, from real measurements (insole length
     per EU size). This cuts size exchanges.
  3. Product videos (the Reels from the calendar) on product pages.
  4. Fit notes: ask every delivered customer by WhatsApp two days after
     delivery.
  5. Collections by use: Office, Everyday, Occasions, once there are enough
     real products.
  6. Abandoned checkout: Shopify sends an email automatically if the
     customer gave an email; with phone only checkout, follow up on WhatsApp
     manually until Watix works.

E. WHAT NOT TO CHANGE
  * Do not add online payment gateways you have not opened accounts with.
    Research found no native Shopify support for JazzCash or Easypaisa (only
    paid custom integrations), so COD stays the only method for now.
  * Do not upgrade the Shopify plan for its own sake. Basic covers a new store.
    Revisit only if a needed feature is limited to a higher plan.
