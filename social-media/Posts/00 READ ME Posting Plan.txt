WHY THESE FORMATS  (sources)
  * 1080x1350 (4:5) is the recommended feed size; the profile grid now
    previews posts at 3:4, so a little of the top and bottom is cropped in
    the grid. All text sits inside a safe margin for that reason.
    https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-post-size
  * Stories are 1080x1920; keep content about 150 px from the top and 250 px
    from the bottom. These designs keep 250 px and 340 px.
  * Carousels (Days 04, 08, 09, 14) teach one idea per slide.

WHAT IS MISSING ON PURPOSE
Product posts and Reels need real photos and video of the real pairs. None
exist yet (the store samples are drawings). When the pairs arrive, follow
the DAUR Photo Guide and send the photos to Claude; product posts, Reels
scripts and product ads will be added as Day 15 onwards.

HONEST LIMITS
Engagement cannot be promised in advance. After 14 days, open Instagram
Professional dashboard and send Claude the reach, saves, shares and profile
